The endpoint-by-endpoint reference (every path, field and response) is in the [playground](/docs/api-reference),
which opens with your sandbox key. This page covers what every integration needs.

## Base URL

| Environment | Base URL |
|---|---|
| Production | `https://api.provd.tech/v1` (placeholder during early access) |
| Local | `http://localhost:8000/v1` |

All requests and responses are JSON. Timestamps are ISO 8601 in UTC.

## Authentication

Calls that score or read your data take your key in the `X-API-Key` header. Public endpoints need no key and are rate-limited per IP.

Every account has two kinds of key:

| Key | Starts with | Use it | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sandbox | `sbx_` | The playground, your tests and CI | Scores fixed test wallets for free. Nothing is charged, no live data is read, records are never anchored, and sandbox attestations are rejected by contracts that trust Provd's live signer. |
| Live | anything else | **Only from your server** | Scores real payments. Live keys are refused when sent from a browser (a request with an `Origin` header), so a key in a web page can't be used. |

Keep both in your secret store, never in code or in an app. The two keys see separate data: sandbox decisions
and outcomes never appear in your live account.

```bash
curl -s https://api.provd.tech/v1/actions/evaluate \
  -H "X-API-Key: $PROVD_API_KEY" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{"chain":"tron","intent_type":"p2p_trade","counterparty":{"wallet_address":"T…"},"rails":{"fiat_rail":"mpesa","crypto_asset":"USDT_TRC20"},"amounts":{"fiat":65000,"fiat_currency":"KES","crypto":500}}'
```

Send an `Idempotency-Key` header on evaluate: retries with the same key return the original decision and are not charged again.

## Errors

| Status | When |
|---|---|
| `401` / `403` | Missing, invalid or inactive API key; a live key sent from a browser; or a top-up with a sandbox key. |
| `402` | Free quota (or monthly plan) used and no balance. The body has `balance`, `units_required`, `purchase_url` and `topup_endpoint`. See [pay as you go](/docs/pay-as-you-go). |
| `404` | Decision or `intent_id` not found for your account. |
| `413` | Request body larger than 64 KB. |
| `422` | Invalid body, invalid address, unsupported chain on the public check, or a reused `intent_id`. Body: `{"detail": "…"}`. |
| `429` | Rate limit reached. Wait for the number of seconds in the `Retry-After` header and retry. |
| `5xx` | Our side, or the chain data provider. Safe to retry with the same `Idempotency-Key`. |

Scored today: `p2p_trade`, `ramp_conversion`, `payout` and `otc_settlement` on `tron` (USDT) and `solana` (USDC and USDT). Chains and intent types we know but don't score yet (`stellar`, `evm`; `swap`, `deposit`, `payment`…) are accepted and return `recommended_action: "not_yet_supported"` with a `NOT_YET_SUPPORTED` flag. They are not charged.

## Limits

| What | Limit |
|---|---|
| Calls with your live key | 600 per minute per key |
| Calls with your sandbox key | 60 per minute per key |
| Public endpoints (counterparty check, pricing, proof) | 120 per minute per IP; the counterparty check 20 per minute |
| Request body | 64 KB |
| `intent_id`, `Idempotency-Key` | Up to 64 characters: letters, digits and `. _ : -` |
| `context` | Up to 4 KB of JSON, 4 levels deep, 50 keys. Stored with the decision, never scored |
| `X-Platform-Context` | Up to 1 KB |

Need higher limits? Monthly plans include them; [contact us](mailto:hello@provd.tech).

## Optional inputs that sharpen a score

| Field | Used by | What to send |
|---|---|---|
| `amounts.crypto` or `amounts.fiat` + `fiat_currency` | `amount_pattern` | The amount. Provd learns your own baselines from it: per counterparty, and your 90-day p99 per intent type. |
| `known_counterparties` | `lookalike_destination` | Addresses you've paid or traded with (your address book, up to 500). Provd also learns them from your released checks. Catches lookalike (address-poisoning) destinations from the first call. |
| `float_available` | `float_and_timing` | Your available float on this rail, in `amounts.fiat_currency` (payouts, OTC). |
| `account_signals` | `account_takeover_proxies` | Timestamps from your SIM-swap, device-trust or KYC provider: `sim_swap_at`, `device_changed_at`, `credentials_changed_at`, `payout_destination_added_at`, `account_created_at`, plus `provider`. Provd doesn't detect these itself; send what you have. |

Signals with nothing to work on report `none` in `signal_coverage` and add no risk.

## Risk scale

| Score | `risk_level` | `recommended_action` |
|---|---|---|
| 0–249 | `low` | `release` |
| 250–499 | `medium` | `manual_review` |
| 500–749 | `high` | `hold` |
| 750–1000 | `critical` | `reject` |

## Flag codes

| Code | Severity | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `SANCTIONED_ADDRESS` | critical | The counterparty wallet is on the US Treasury OFAC sanctions (SDN) list; the reason names the listed entity. |
| `SANCTIONS_EXPOSURE` | high | The wallet transacted directly with addresses on the OFAC sanctions list. Never discounted as incidental. |
| `CP_ADDRESS_FROZEN` | critical | The counterparty wallet is itself on a public risk list (for example, frozen by Tether). |
| `CP_FLAGGED_EXPOSURE` | high | The wallet transacted directly with wallets on a public risk list. |
| `CP_INCIDENTAL_EXPOSURE` | medium | Exposure to listed wallets is a small share of volume across many counterparties, as with exchanges. |
| `CP_PLATFORM_FLAGGED_EXPOSURE` | high | The wallet transacted with wallets you confirmed as fraudulent. |
| `CP_NEW_WALLET` | low | First USDT activity in the last 7 days, with few transfers. |
| `CP_NO_HISTORY` | low | No USDT transfers found for this wallet. |
| `OWN_FRAUD_HISTORY` | critical | You reported confirmed fraud or a fake payment confirmation from this counterparty in the last 180 days. |
| `OWN_REVERSAL_HISTORY` | medium or high | You reported reversals, refunds or chargebacks from this counterparty in the last 180 days. |
| `VELOCITY_SPIKE` | medium | Unusually many checks for this counterparty in the last 24 hours. |
| `LOOKALIKE_DESTINATION` | high or critical | The destination shares its first and last characters with an address you've paid, but is a different address: the address-poisoning trick. Critical when 4+ characters match at both ends. |
| `DUST_SENDER` | high | The destination only sends tiny "dust" amounts to other wallets and never receives real value, typical of poisoning bots. |
| `AMOUNT_SPIKE` | medium or high | Much larger (5x+, 20x+ for high) than this counterparty's largest previous amount with you. |
| `AMOUNT_ABOVE_PLATFORM_P99` | medium | Larger than 99% of your amounts for this intent type over 90 days (needs 30+ decisions). |
| `AMOUNT_STRUCTURING` | high | Three or more amounts just under a reporting threshold for this counterparty in 24 hours. |
| `RAPID_PASS_THROUGH` | high | Funds covering this amount arrived in the wallet within the last hour. |
| `FLOAT_STRAIN` | medium | The amount uses more than half of your available float. |
| `OFF_HOURS` | low | Fewer than 2% of your checks happen at this UTC hour. |
| `SIM_SWAP_RECENT` | high or critical | SIM swap in the last 72 hours; critical within 24 hours together with a new payout destination. |
| `DEVICE_CHANGE_WITH_ACCOUNT_CHANGE` | high | New device in the last 24 hours plus a new payout destination or credential change. |
| `NEW_PAYOUT_DESTINATION` | low | Payout destination added in the last 72 hours. |
| `NEW_ACCOUNT` | medium | Account opened in the last 24 hours. |
| `TOKEN_HONEYPOT`, `TOKEN_BLACKLIST_FUNCTION`, `TOKEN_MINTABLE`, `TOKEN_PAUSABLE` | varies | Risky token contract properties (`ramp_conversion` only). |
| `REDUCED_SIGNAL_COVERAGE` | info | A data source was unavailable, so a signal ran on partial data. |
| `NOT_YET_SUPPORTED` | info | The chain or intent type isn't scored yet. |

## Sanctions screening

Every check screens the wallet and its direct counterparties against the digital currency addresses on the US
Treasury OFAC SDN list (Tron, Solana and EVM addresses), refreshed daily from the official publication. A match is a
strong risk signal for your own compliance process, not a legal determination, and other lists (UN, EU, UK) aren't
included yet.

## Free lookalike check

`POST /v1/lookalike` needs no key (rate-limited like the counterparty check). Send the address you're about to pay
and the addresses you've paid before; get back whether it imitates one of them and whether it behaves like a
poisoning bot. The same check runs inside every scored request as `lookalike_destination`.

```bash
curl -s https://api.provd.tech/v1/lookalike -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{"chain":"solana","address":"EKYWHE68zW33SkFNmcqPneowxagcsgjpqYmLVs4yvJKC","known":["EKYWxPNqtscvebruQdj7HkPL6CyFLJamwNnrLgi1vJKC"]}'
```

## Release attestations

`POST /v1/decisions/{intent_id}/attestation` returns a signed release a smart contract can verify on-chain: EIP-712
for EVM and Tron (TVM) contracts, a keccak layout for Solana programs. Only for `release` decisions under 24 hours
old, for the scored wallet and at most the scored amount, valid for up to one hour, bound to one contract or program.
See [Non-custodial and escrow releases](/docs/non-custodial).

## Decision records

`GET /v1/decisions/{intent_id}` returns the signed record behind a score, with its Merkle proof and Bitcoin anchor once sealed. `merkle` and `batch` are `null` until the record joins a batch (within minutes). See [decision records](/docs/decisions), or paste a record into [Verify a decision](/verify).
