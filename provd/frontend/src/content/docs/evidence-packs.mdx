---
title: Compliance evidence packs
description: Show a regulator, a bank or an auditor how you screened every release over a period, with signed records they can verify themselves.
---

Licensed platforms are asked the same questions everywhere: do you screen for sanctions, do you monitor transactions,
do you report suspicious ones, and can you prove it from your records? An evidence pack answers all four for any
period, from the checks you already run through Provd.

## What's in a pack

- **Coverage:** how many releases were checked before the funds left, and how many were released, held, rejected or
  sent to review, by chain, type and asset.
- **Exceptions:** every hold, reject and review, with the reasons and the outcome you reported afterwards.
- **Draft suspicious-transaction reports** for releases you refused on critical grounds (for example a sanctioned or
  frozen wallet) and for releases you later confirmed as fraud. Each draft carries the facts from the decision record:
  wallet, amount, date, indicators, the action taken and the evidence. Your compliance officer completes the
  subject's details from your KYC and files it through your financial intelligence unit's channel (goAML in many
  countries).
- **How this evidences each obligation**, mapped to the **FATF Recommendations** by default: targeted financial
  sanctions (R.6, R.7), ongoing monitoring (R.10), suspicious transaction reporting (R.20) and record keeping (R.11),
  as applied to virtual asset service providers by R.15. Jurisdiction profiles add the local instruments (today:
  `kenya`).
- **Every decision record** for the period, signed and anchored to Bitcoin, exportable for your retention archive.
- **A signature over the whole pack**, so anyone can tell it hasn't been edited.

Drafts are drafts and the mapping isn't legal advice: confirm your obligations with your advisers.

## Get a pack

A printable report (open it and print to PDF):

```bash
curl -s "https://api.provd.tech/v1/evidence?from=2026-07-01&to=2026-09-30&format=html" \
  -H "X-API-Key: $PROVD_API_KEY" -o evidence-q3.html
```

The same pack as JSON, mapped to a jurisdiction profile:

```bash
curl -s "https://api.provd.tech/v1/evidence?from=2026-07-01&to=2026-09-30&framework=kenya" \
  -H "X-API-Key: $PROVD_API_KEY"
```

Every record in the period, one per line, for your archive:

```bash
curl -s "https://api.provd.tech/v1/evidence/records?from=2026-07-01&to=2026-09-30" \
  -H "X-API-Key: $PROVD_API_KEY" -o records-q3.jsonl
```

- `from` and `to` are UTC dates, both included; a pack covers up to 400 days (default: the last 30).
- Call it from your server with your live key. With your sandbox key you get a pack of your sandbox checks, marked as
  sandbox data.
- Packs are free and rate-limited (30 an hour).

## Make packs complete

A pack can only show what Provd saw:

- **Check every release,** not a sample: coverage is the first thing an examiner reads.
- **Report outcomes** (`POST /v1/outcomes`): reversals, chargebacks, confirmed fraud and fake payment confirmations.
  Confirmed fraud turns into a draft report, and outcomes sharpen your future checks.
- **Send the amount and asset** with each check, so volumes and unusual-amount checks are right.

## Verify a pack

The `signature` covers the SHA-256 of the pack's canonical JSON (sorted keys, no whitespace) without the `signature`
field, signed with Provd's key from `GET /v1/proof/keys`. Each exported record verifies on its own, in the browser at
[Verify a decision](/verify) or with the steps in [Decision records](/docs/decisions).
