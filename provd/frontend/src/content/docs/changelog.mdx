## 2026-10-05

- Compliance evidence packs: `GET /v1/evidence` returns screening coverage, every hold and reject with its reasons and outcome, draft suspicious-transaction reports and a mapping to the FATF Recommendations (or a jurisdiction profile) for any period, signed, as JSON or a printable report. `GET /v1/evidence/records` exports every signed record in the period.

- Self-serve sandbox keys: get a free `sbx_` key instantly at provd.tech/early-access or in the playground, no call needed. Live keys are still issued by Provd.

## 2026-10-04

- Sandbox keys (`sbx_…`): free checks against fixed test wallets on Tron and Solana (clean, lookalike, sanctioned, exposure, frozen, dust, new), isolated from your live account, never charged or anchored. Sandbox records say `"environment": "sandbox"` and sandbox attestations are signed by a separate address, so live contracts reject them.
- Live keys now work only from your server: requests with a live key from a browser (with an `Origin` header) get `403`.
- The docs moved to docs.provd.tech, and the endpoint reference to playground.provd.tech, which opens with your sandbox key and lets you try each call.

- Release attestations: `POST /v1/decisions/{intent_id}/attestation` signs a release that an escrow contract verifies on-chain (EIP-712 on EVM and Tron; keccak layout on Solana). Solidity verifier and example escrow in the repository; signer address at `GET /v1/proof/keys`.
- Sanctions screening: wallets and their direct counterparties are screened against the US Treasury OFAC SDN list (`SANCTIONED_ADDRESS`, `SANCTIONS_EXPOSURE`), refreshed daily.
- Solana: checks for USDC and USDT on Solana (`"chain": "solana"`), including Circle's and Tether's frozen accounts on Solana.
- Lookalike destinations: a new signal, `lookalike_destination`, catches address poisoning on Solana and Tron (`LOOKALIKE_DESTINATION`, `DUST_SENDER`). Send `known_counterparties` or let Provd learn your payees from released checks. Free public version: `POST /v1/lookalike` and provd.tech/check#lookalike.
- M-Pesa top-ups now go through Paystack checkout (`pay_url`) and are credited automatically; send an `email` with `"method": "mpesa"`.
- Card top-ups: `POST /v1/topups` with `"method": "card"` and an `email` returns a Paystack checkout link (`pay_url`). Checks are credited when Paystack confirms the charge. `POST /v1/topups/{id}/refresh` checks a card payment on demand.
- Pricing: the free tier is now 300 checks a month (was 100 a day). New `1k` bundle: 1,000 checks for $20 / KES 2,600. `GET /v1/usage` returns `free_monthly_checks`, `free_used_this_month` and `free_remaining_this_month` in place of the daily fields.
- `rails.fiat_rail` and `amounts.fiat_currency` take any local rail and currency (for example `pix`/`BRL`, `upi`/`INR`); amount baselines are learned per currency.
- Monthly plans for global payout platforms: Payouts Starter, Payouts Pro and Enterprise, listed in `GET /v1/pricing` under `plans`.

## 2026-10-03

- Provd now scores money movement on African rails before funds are released. First coverage: USDT on Tron, with M-Pesa and bank rails as fiat context.
- New API under `/v1`: `POST /v1/actions/evaluate`, `GET /v1/decisions/{intent_id}`, `POST /v1/outcomes`, `GET /v1/counterparties/tron/{address}`, `GET /v1/usage`, `GET /v1/pricing`, `POST /v1/topups`, `GET /v1/topups`.
- Pay as you go: 100 free checks a day, then prepaid bundles topped up with USDT (TRC-20) or M-Pesa.
- Public [counterparty check](/check) on the website.
- Earlier APIs under `/api/v1` and their documentation have been retired.
