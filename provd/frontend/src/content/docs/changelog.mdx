## 2026-10-02

- Provd is now security-posture monitoring for cross-chain protocols. First coverage: LayerZero V2 OApps and OFTs on Ethereum.
- New public API: `GET /api/v1/protocols`, `GET /api/v1/protocols/{slug}`, `POST /api/v1/scan`, `POST /api/v1/early-access`.
- Published the rating [methodology](/methodology) and our first research note, [Kelp DAO: the exploited route ran on one verifier for 381 days](/blog/kelp-dao-381-days).
- Previous APIs and documentation have been retired.
